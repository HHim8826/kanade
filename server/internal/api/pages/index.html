Kanade

A personal, self-hosted music library and player.

What Kanade does

Kanade is a music app run by its owner for their own use. It helps the owner:

  • import music files and albums, including Japanese releases with original-language titles;
  • organize tracks, albums, artists and versions, and remove exact duplicate files;
  • stream the music to the owner's phone and computer, with playlists, favorites and play history.

Why Kanade uses Google Drive

Kanade keeps the owner's music files in the owner's own Google Drive, so the library does not depend on a single server's disk. After the owner signs in with Google and grants access, Kanade uses the Google Drive API to:

  • upload imported music files, cover art and lyrics to the owner's Drive;
  • list and read those files to build the music library;
  • stream audio from Drive to the owner's devices for playback.

Data from Google Drive is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Details are in the Kanade Privacy Policy.
