Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

What this app is

Kanade is a self-hosted, single-user music library operated by its owner at music.ser1ka.com. It is not offered to other users.

Google user data accessed

With the Google Drive scope, the app lists, reads, uploads and organizes audio files and related files (cover art, cue sheets, lyrics) in the owner's Google Drive, and streams those files to the owner's own devices.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and the library index (file IDs, tags, playlists, play history) are stored only on the owner's server. Tokens are never sent to client devices or to any third party.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or analytics. If the owner enables optional metadata lookups, only tags or audio fingerprints are sent to music metadata services; Drive files themselves are never sent.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's server data removes the stored tokens and library index; files in Google Drive are not affected.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
